## 1. Business Purpose & Client Use Cases

This digital platform is a modern, high-converting cinematic production studio website and self-service booking portal engineered to establish brand authority and drive direct client acquisition.

### Key Workflows & Examples

| Client Category | How the Website Serves Them | Business Advantage |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Luxury Weddings & Galas** | Couples review 4K cinema packages, select dates in their local timezone, and submit deposit bookings. | Streamlined reservation with instant 25% deposit calculations eliminates back-and-forth booking delays. |
| **High-Fashion & Runway Stills** | Editorial clients review studio specs (Profoto lighting, cyclorama cove), select studio locations, and book shoot slots. | Highlights luxury technical capabilities and transparent base day rates. |
| **Commercial Brands & Agencies** | Brand producers use the interactive rate calculator with drone & 8K cinema upgrades. | Provides instant, itemized rate cards with automated tax and location fees. |
| **Client Portal & Tracking** | Booked clients log into a dedicated portal to track production progress from Pre-Production to Final Master Delivery. | Professional self-service experience elevates client confidence and retention. |

## 2. Technology Stack & Design System

### Technical Architecture
- **Frontend Core:** React 19 + TypeScript (strict mode) for rock-solid stability and type safety.
- **Build & Optimization:** Vite 8 for fast sub-250ms production builds.
- **Visual Engine:** Tailwind CSS v4 + Framer Motion for hardware-accelerated micro-interactions.
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Continuous integration and global CDN edge distribution via Vercel.

### Design Tokens & Aesthetics
- **Void Black (`#0a0a0a`):** Deep theater-grade cinematic background providing optimal visual depth.
- **Obsidian Gold (`#c8a96e`):** Premium luxury accent for call-to-action buttons, active highlights, and badges.
- **Anamorphic Ember (`#e05a3a`):** Dynamic accent for live indicators, notifications, and alerts.
- **High-Contrast Typography (`#f5f5f5` / `#a0a0a0`):** Engineered for full WCAG AA readability compliance across desktop and mobile screens.
